Welcome "Sur le Rocher" (On The Rock), in the Principality of Monaco

Top three activities to do in Monte-Carlo

Play at the Grand Casino De Monte-Carlo

Put your poker face skills to the test or go undercover 007.
Fun Fact: it is forbidden for Monegasque nationals to enter the Casino

Attend the Annual Monaco Grand-Prix

If engine sounds, scorching heat and grand events are your thing, then attend the Grand Prix. Be sure to wear shorts and have a spot on the top of the Starbucks in La Condamine.
Fun Fact: you might run into Lewis Hamilton, he is a resident of Monaco, he is currently driving for Scuderia Ferrari
